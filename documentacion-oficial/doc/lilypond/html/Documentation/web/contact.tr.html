LilyPond Wiki

The LilyPond Wiki, successor of the LilyPond Snippet Repository (LSR), is a large collection of user-submitted examples that can be freely copied and used in your own works. See what other people have written, and add your own!

https://wiki.lilypond.community

Particularly instructive examples from LilyPond Wiki are included in our official documentation, in Snippets.
